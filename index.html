Research Interests

IR modifications of gravity

Studying infrared deviations and large-scale cosmic acceleration dynamics beyond General Relativity.

Matter-Geometry couplings

Investigating non-minimal couplings between matter fields and geometric curvatures in gravitational actions.

Weyl geometry in Gravity

Exploring conformal invariance, non-metricity, and Weyl geometry formulations of gravity.

Finsler Spaces in Gravity

Utilizing Finslerian manifolds and directional metric structures in advanced gravitational cosmology.
