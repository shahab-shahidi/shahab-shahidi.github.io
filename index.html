Research Interests

IR modifications of gravity

Infrared extensions and modifications to General Relativity on cosmological and Gpc scales.

Matter-Geometry couplings

Non-minimal interactions between matter fields and geometric curvatures in extended gravities.

Weyl geometry in Gravity

Conformal invariance, non-metricity tensors, and Weyl geometry frameworks in cosmic dynamics.

Finsler Spaces in Gravity

Geometric foundations based on Finslerian manifolds and directional metric structures.
