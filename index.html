Dr. Shahab Shahidi

Associate Professor of Theoretical Physics and Computational Cosmologist. My research explores dark energy dynamics, modified theories of gravity, relativistic astrophysics, and statistical cosmological inference.

Research Interests

Core areas of theoretical investigation and numerical exploration.

Cosmology & Dark Energy

Investigating late-time cosmic acceleration, cosmological perturbation theory, and dynamical dark energy models.

Modified Gravity

Developing and constraining scalar-tensor frameworks, extended gravitational actions, and alternative cosmic evolution scenarios.

Relativistic Astrophysics

Studying compact objects, baryonic matter equations of state, and relativistic stellar configurations.

Python & Numerical MCMC

Implementing Boltzmann solvers, numerical optimization routines, and Markov Chain Monte Carlo parameter inference.
