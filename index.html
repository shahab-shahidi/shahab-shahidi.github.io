Research Interests

IR Modifications of Gravity

Studying infrared modifications to General Relativity to address large-scale cosmic acceleration and gravitational dynamics.

Matter-Geometry Couplings

Investigating non-minimal interactions between matter fields and geometric invariants in gravitational actions.

Weyl Geometry in Gravity

Exploring conformal geometry extensions, non-metricity, and Weyl-invariant formulations of cosmological models.

Finsler Spaces in Gravity

Utilizing Finslerian geometry and directional-dependent metrics for advanced gravitational and cosmological frameworks.
