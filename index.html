Research Interests

IR modifications of gravity

Infrared deviations from General Relativity and cosmic expansion dynamics on large scales.

Matter-Geometry couplings

Investigating non-minimal couplings between matter fields and geometric invariants.

Dynamical DE models

Dynamical Dark Energy formulations, scalar fields, and cosmological evolution scenarios.

Weyl geometry in Gravity

Conformal invariance, gauge structures, non-metricity, and Weyl gravity formulations.

Finsler Spaces in Gravity

Directional metric spaces, geometric generalizations, and anisotropy in relativistic systems.
